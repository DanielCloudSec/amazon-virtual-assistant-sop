# VA Onboarding Checklist

1. Sign NDA and independent contractor agreement
2. Create company Gmail account and grant access to:
   - Google Sheets
   - Google Drive (Training folder, Product listings)
   - Slack channels (Listings, Training, Announcements)
   - Setup Hubstaff account for time logging
3. Watch orientation video (link to YouTube)
4. Review and confirm access to:
   - Amazon Seller Central
   - Smartsheet task dashboard
   - Product listings folder
5. First assignment:
   - Review competitor listings Google Sheet
   - Fill out SEO worksheet
   - Use Python script to find best products for futher evaluation
   - Enter product into Smartsheet to get "Listing Score"
